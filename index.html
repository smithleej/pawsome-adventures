Garden Dash

Help Cracker, Charlie, Aurelia and Bonnie collect 12 golden paws, solve four linked pet challenges and open the garden gate! Explore the side paths for four optional hidden treasures — one for each pet.

Move with the joystick or WASD / arrows. Jump with the button or Space. Cracker double-jumps with Space twice. Charlie crouches with E. Aurelia pushes crates by walking into them. Bonnie follows scents and digs with E. Switch pets with 1–4.
